📌 LEETCODE 1610 — MASTER NOTES (Anubhav Edition)
✅ Problem in Simple Words

Tu ek point par khada hai (location).
Tere paas bahut saare points hain.

Tu apna face ghumaa sakta hai,
lekin ek baar me sirf angle degree ke andar hi dekh sakta hai.

👉 Task:
Aisi direction choose karo jahan maximum points dikhen.

✅ Real Life Analogy

Soch tu torch lekar khada hai.

Torch ka cone = angle

Tu torch ghumata hai

Jo log torch ke cone me aate hain wo dikhenge

👉 Goal: torch ko aise rakho ki sabse zyada log roshni me aa jaayein.

✅ Core Idea

Har point ko dekhne ke liye
hume kitna ghoomna padega — wo nikaalte hain.

👉 Is number ko angle kehte hain.

Phir hume aisa group chahiye jisme:

maxAngle - minAngle <= viewAngle


Aur size max ho.

✅ Step 1 — Convert Points → Angles

Har point (x, y) ke liye:

dx = x - location.x
dy = y - location.y
angle = atan2(dy, dx)


👉 atan2 batata hai direction.

✅ atan2 kya karta hai?

atan2(y, x) batata hai:

👉 X-axis se kitna ghoomna padega us point ko dekhne ke liye.

Examples:

(1,0) → 0°
(1,1) → 45°
(0,1) → 90°
(-1,0) → 180°


Isliye hum tan use nahi karte — atan2 safe hai.

✅ Step 2 — Same Location Points

Agar koi point exactly location par hai:

dx == 0 && dy == 0


👉 Wo hamesha dikhega.
👉 Usko alag count karo.

✅ Step 3 — SORT kyon?

Angles random hote hain:

[120, 10, 300, 40]


Sort ke baad:

[10, 40, 120, 300]


👉 Jo same direction ke paas hain, wo paas-paas aa jaate hain.

Rule:

Jab bhi range / window / difference chahiye → SORT.

✅ Step 4 — Circular Problem

Angles circle par hote hain:

0° ----> 360° ----> 0°


Example:

350° and 10°


Actually paas hain.

But normal subtract fail karega.

✅ Step 5 — Duplicate (+360)

Circle ko line banane ke liye:

Har angle ko dubara daalo +360 karke.

Example:

[10,40,120,300]
→ [10,40,120,300,370,400,480,660]


👉 Isse 300 → 10 continuous ban jaata hai.

Rule:

Circular data → duplicate → sliding window.

✅ Step 6 — Sliding Window

Two pointers:

l = 0
r = 0


Window valid tab hai jab:

angles[r] - angles[l] <= viewAngle


Agar cross ho jaaye:

angles[r] - angles[l] > viewAngle


👉 left badhao.

✅ Why condition is > angle?

Hum galat window ko fix karte hain.

while (angles[r] - angles[l] > angle)
    l++;


👉 Jab window zyada wide ho jaaye tab shrink.

✅ Pattern Recognition (Interview Gold)

Jab dikhe:

direction

angle

circular

maximum in range

Socho:

Convert → Sort → Duplicate → Sliding Window

✅ Time & Space
Time: O(n log n)
Space: O(n)
